## Why

A coach who reads the log is more useful than a chat that remembers the conversation. Rack gives the model tools over the same tables the tap logger writes to, and a knowledge base where every claim carries a strength tag: well-replicated, reasonable consensus, or practitioner heuristic. The numeric floors and the injury and disordered-eating boundaries are written down and take precedence.
