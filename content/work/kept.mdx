## Why

Most people have one relative whose stories they mean to record and never do. Kept is a gift: the giver buys it, the storyteller gets a link and a set of questions, and answers by voice or video on their own time. Follow-up questions come from what they said. The family keeps the recordings.

If the storyteller passes, the family gets a memorial book at no cost. Pricing has a hardship tier.
