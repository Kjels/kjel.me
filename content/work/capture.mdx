## Why

Most of what I want to write down is not a task and not a note, it is a thought that will become one of those later. Capture takes the thought as text or voice and lets a model decide, silently, whether it is a todo, a note, or a reminder. No conversation. Input goes in, structure comes out, and nothing is ever dropped: unclassified text is kept as a note.

## What it is

One endpoint, many surfaces. A web app with keyboard-first views for today, upcoming and notes. A Raycast extension. An iOS shortcut. A watcher that posts Superwhisper transcripts by mode name. A native macOS menu-bar app with a global hotkey that runs the same prompt against a local database.

The useful distinction it makes is between when something is due and when it should resurface. Those are different dates.

## Where it stands

Working personal tool. Two runtimes coexist and one has to win before it can be called a framework. The classification prompt and the tool loop are the reusable parts.
