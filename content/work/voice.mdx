## Why

Every draft an assistant writes for you is a little off. The fix is not a better prompt, it is a record of the difference: what it suggested, what you actually sent. Keep that delta long enough and the drafts start sounding like you.

## What it would be

Claude connected to Gmail. A tuning mode that watches the emails you send and diffs them against what it proposed. A small script that learns how you type, not just what you say.
